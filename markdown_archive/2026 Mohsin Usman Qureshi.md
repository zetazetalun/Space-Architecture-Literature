## Abstract
Sulphur, a major by-product of the oil and gas industry, has emerged as a promising construction material. This review examines the development, properties, and uses of sulphur-based construction materials over a century. Key advantages of sulphur concrete include rapid strength gain (~50 MPa within 1–2 days) and exceptional chemical durability in extreme environments. The review also summarizes innovative applications ranging from acid-resistant industrial structures to sustainable pavement materials and even extraterrestrial construction. Future research directions include advancing 3D printing techniques and field validation for long-term performance.

## 3. Discussion on Concrete and Asphalt Applications

### 3.1. Materials and Processing
Sulphur concrete uses elemental sulphur (S8) as the binder. It is heated above its melting point (~119 °C) to form a low-viscosity liquid and mixed with aggregates at 130–145 °C. Unlike Portland cement, it gains strength upon cooling rather than hydration, eliminating curing requirements.

### 3.4. Applications and Case Studies (Extraterrestrial Construction)
- **Moon and Mars Habitat Modules:** Recent research suggests using sulphur (abundant in lunar/Martian regolith) as a binder to produce concrete-like blocks for habitats.
- **NASA Studies:** Sulphur regolith concrete with compressive strength ~25 MPa has been studied. 3D-printed structures using robotic systems have been demonstrated for scenarios where water is scarce.
- **Rationale:** In situ resource utilisation (ISRU), no water required, fast setting for robotic construction, and good performance in vacuum/low-temperature environments.

## 6. Conclusions
Sulphur-based materials represent a compelling paradigm shift. For extraterrestrial construction, they provide a waterless, ISRU-based solution that is essential for lunar and Martian concepts. Developments in automation, thermal control, and rapid manufacturing are accelerating its transition from a niche alternative to a platform material for advanced construction.

# Assessing the Efficiency of Integrating BIM and Blockchain to Improve Information Management for Mars Buildings: A SWOT-AHP Analysis

**Authors:** Amirhossein Javaherikhah, Mercedes Valiente Lopez, Hadi Sarvari  
**Journal:** Buildings 2025, 15, 494  
**Date:** Published 5 February 2025  

## Abstract
This research investigates integrating Building Information Modeling (BIM) and blockchain technology to enhance building information’s security, reliability, and accuracy in Martian environments. Evaluated through a SWOT-AHP analysis with expert input, the study identifies strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats. Findings indicate that while cost calculation and budgeting are major strengths (26.21 score), technological complexity (25.488) remains a primary weakness. The SWOT analysis positions the integration in a conservative strategy quadrant, suggesting the technology is promising but requires further refinement for Martian habitats.

## 1. Introduction
Mars presents extreme challenges: soil toxicity, intense radiation (requiring ~1m regolith shielding), and low gravity (38% of Earth's). Sustainable dwellings require advanced management. BIM allows precise design, while blockchain ensures immutable data for tracking resources and materials (ISRU).

## 2. Research Background
- **BIM & Blockchain:** Ensures modeling accuracy, resource assurance, and continuous data monitoring. 
- **Martian Constraints:** Lack of infrastructure, distance from Earth, and extreme temperature fluctuations (-60°C) complicate technology transfer.

## 3. Results (SWOT-AHP Analysis)

### Strengths (S)
- **S1:** Detailed modeling and construction instructions (Weight: 0.07)
- **S3:** Cost calculation and budgeting (Score: 26.21; Weight: 0.01)
- **S6:** High precision (Weight: 0.06)

### Weaknesses (W)
- **W1:** Complexity of technology (Score: 25.488; Weight: 0.25)
- **W4:** Limited infrastructure (Weight: 0.27)

### Opportunities (O)
- **O1:** Innovation in construction (Weight: 0.23)
- **O3:** Increased productivity (Score: 19.16; Weight: 0.18)

### Threats (T)
- **T2:** Defects in data security (Score: 20.68; Weight: 0.34)
- **T3:** Difficult environmental conditions (Weight: 0.25)

## 4. Conclusion
The integration of BIM and blockchain is essential for addressing the precision needed for Martian buildings. However, the study concludes it currently sits in a 'Conservative Strategy' quadrant due to significant hurdles in Martian infrastructure and technical complexity. Overcoming these is critical for realizing autonomous construction systems.

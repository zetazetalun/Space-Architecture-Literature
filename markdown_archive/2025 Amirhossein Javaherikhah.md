# Assessing the Efficiency of Integrating BIM and Blockchain to Improve Information Management for Mars Buildings: A SWOT-AHP Analysis

**Authors:** Amirhossein Javaherikhah, Mercedes Valiente Lopez, and Hadi Sarvari
**Published:** 5 February 2025
**Journal:** Buildings 2025, 15, 494
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.3390/buildings15030494

## Abstract
This research investigates integrating Building Information Modeling (BIM) and blockchain technology to enhance building information’s security, reliability, and accuracy in Martian environments. Given the unique challenges posed by extraterrestrial construction, this study evaluates the feasibility of this hybrid approach through a structured SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats) analysis. Expert inputs were collected through a comprehensive questionnaire identifying nine strengths, eight weaknesses, eight opportunities, and six threats. The Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP) was used to prioritize these factors.

## 1. Introduction
Mars presents acute challenges for inhabitation, including soil toxicity, intense radiation, and low gravity. Managing information and construction processes on this planet requires advanced technologies. BIM allows for accurate designs and simulation, while blockchain provides transparent and secure data recording. Together, they optimize construction processes and material tracking.

## 2. Research Background
BIM and blockchain integration can lead to accurate modeling of building dimensions and assurance of construction instructions. However, implementation on Mars faces issues such as lack of basic infrastructure, technology stability, and time-consuming transfers from Earth. Previous research has not specifically examined the implementation method for Martian environments using a SWOT-AHP framework.

## 4. Analysis and Findings
The AHP analysis prioritized the factors as follows:
- **Top Strength:** Cost calculation and budgeting (Weight: 26.21).
- **Top Weakness:** Technology complexity (Weight: 25.488).
- **Top Opportunity:** Increased productivity (Weight: 19.16).
- **Top Threat:** Defects in data security (Weight: 20.68).

The SWOT analysis (Figure 11) places the BIM and blockchain integration strategy in the **conservative quadrant** (Score: -0.038, 0.009). This indicates the technology holds promise but requires refinement and better infrastructure before deployment.

## 5. Conclusions
The integration of BIM and blockchain is crucial for addressing the unique challenges of Martian buildings, providing precision and dependability. However, significant obstacles remain, particularly limited infrastructure and high technological complexity. Future research should focus on constructing frameworks for Martian construction information and exploring novel approaches to improve reliability.

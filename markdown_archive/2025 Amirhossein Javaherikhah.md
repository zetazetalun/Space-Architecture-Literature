# Assessing the Efficiency of Integrating BIM and Blockchain to Improve Information Management for Mars Buildings: A SWOT-AHP Analysis

**Authors:** Amirhossein Javaherikhah, Mercedes Valiente Lopez, Hadi Sarvari  
**Journal:** Buildings  
**Published:** 5 February 2025  
**DOI:** 10.3390/buildings15030494

## Abstract
This research investigates integrating Building Information Modeling (BIM) and blockchain technology to enhance building information’s security, reliability, and accuracy in Martian environments. Using a structured SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats) analysis and the Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP), the study evaluates the feasibility of this hybrid approach for extraterrestrial construction. Findings suggest that while the technology holds promise for cost calculation and productivity, complexity and lack of infrastructure remain significant barriers.

## 1. Introduction
Mars presents unique challenges for habitation, including soil toxicity, intense radiation, and low gravity. Constructing sustainable dwellings requires advanced management solutions. BIM allows for accurate design simulation, while blockchain provides transparent, immutable data management. Together, they optimize construction processes and material tracking essential for limited-resource environments.

## 3. Findings & Results
- **Strategic Positioning:** The integration falls into the "Conservative Strategy" quadrant, meaning it is promising but requires substantial refinement before deployment.
- **Structural Requirements:** Martian habitats must account for 38% of Earth's gravity and require ~1m of regolith for radiation shielding.
- **Management Efficiency:** BIM-Blockchain integration improves the accuracy of manufacturing instructions and resource management, which are vital for autonomous construction.

## 4. Conclusion
The study provides a foundation for developing flexible and autonomous building systems for Mars. It highlights that the most critical obstacle is the current lack of infrastructure. Future research should focus on robust security policies and adapting construction techniques to the Martian climate.

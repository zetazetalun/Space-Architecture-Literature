# Envisioning Extraterrestrial Construction and the Future Construction Workforce: A Collective Perspective

**Authors:** Amirhosein Jafari, Yimin Zhu, Suniti Karunatillake, Jennifer Qian, Shinhee Jeong, Ali Kazemian, Andrew Webb
**Venue:** 41st International Symposium on Automation and Robotics in Construction (ISARC 2024)

## Abstract
Humanity’s evolution toward an interplanetary species poses a new frontier for the construction industry: extraterrestrial construction. This paper summarizes the outcomes of an interdisciplinary workshop focused on future work (extraterrestrial construction), future workers (extraterrestrial workforce), and future technology (emerging technologies for workforce training). It emphasizes the shift from manual labor to technology-intensive, robot-assisted operations in extreme environments like the Moon and Mars.

## 1. Introduction
NASA and ESA plan to establish permanent human habitats on the Moon and Mars by 2040. Extraterrestrial construction is defined as the theory and practice of building anthropogenic environments in outer space. These environments require automated processes and in-situ resource utilization (ISRU) to be sustainable.

## 2. Methodology
A two-day virtual interdisciplinary workshop titled “Roadmap to Future-ready Extraterrestrial Construction Workforce Development” was organized in February 2023, involving 26 experts from diverse fields including robotics, planetary geoscience, and construction management.

## 3. Summary of Outcomes

### 3.1 Theme 1: Extraterrestrial Construction
*   **Site Conditions:** Significant differences exist between Earth and planetary bodies (radiation, gravity, vacuum, abrasive regolith).
*   **ISRU Necessity:** Transporting materials from Earth is cost-prohibitive; local materials are essential.
*   **Robot-Intensive Work:** Construction will be robot-driven but requires "human-in-the-loop" for troubleshooting, repairs, and creative oversight.
*   **Human-Robot Collaboration (HRC):** A salient feature for all phases, from landing platforms to complex habitats.

### 3.2 Theme 2: Extraterrestrial Construction Workforce
*   **Skill Sets:** Workers need situational awareness, understanding of planetary geology, teleoperation expertise, and mechatronics knowledge.
*   **Challenges:** Isolation, communication latency (e.g., 20 mins between Earth and Mars), and trust in autonomous systems.

### 3.3 Theme 3: Emerging Technologies for Training
*   **AI-XR Training:** Immersive training environments using Artificial Intelligence and Extended Reality are necessary to simulate low-gravity tasks and sensory deprivation.
*   **Evaluation:** Tools like the NASA Task Load Index (TLX) and performance metrics analysis are suggested for assessing trainee readiness.

## 4. Conclusions and Takeaways
*   Extraterrestrial construction requires optimized robotic processes.
*   Human-in-the-loop robot operations are vital for error management.
*   Traditional training is insufficient; hands-on, real-world simulations via AI-XR are required.

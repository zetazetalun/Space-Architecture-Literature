# Envisioning Extraterrestrial Construction and the Future Construction Workforce: A Collective Perspective

**Amirhosein Jafari, Yimin Zhu, Suniti Karunatillake, Jennifer Qian, Shinhee Jeong, Ali Kazemian, Andrew Webb**

## Abstract
Humanity's evolution toward an interplanetary species poses a new frontier for the construction industry: extraterrestrial construction. This paper summarizes the outcomes of a national interdisciplinary workshop structured around three core themes: future work (extraterrestrial construction), future workers (workforce skills), and future technology (training via AI-XR).

## 1 Introduction
NASA and ESA plan to establish permanent human habitats on the Moon and Mars by 2040. Extraterrestrial construction is defined as building anthropogenic environments in outer space. Automated construction processes optimized for these environments, particularly involving in-situ resource utilization (ISRU), are essential for sustainability.

## 2 Summary of Outcomes
### 4.1 Theme 1: Extraterrestrial Construction
* **Site Conditions:** Radiation, gravity differences, vacuum, and abrasive regolith are key factors.
* **ISRU:** In-situ Resource Utilization is a necessity due to high transport costs from Earth.
* **Robot-Intensive:** While autonomous for many tasks, construction will require human-in-the-loop oversight for troubleshooting and repair.
* **Human-Robot Collaboration (HRC):** Teleoperation is a primary mode of construction, though restricted by communication latency (e.g., 20 mins for Mars).

### 4.2 Theme 2: Extraterrestrial Construction Workforce
Required skills include situational awareness, geological knowledge, teleoperation proficiency, and trust in autonomous systems. Workers must be cross-trained in mechatronics, electrical engineering, and programming.

### 4.3 Theme 3: Emerging Technologies for Training
AI-XR (AI and Extended Reality) is the recommended medium for immersive training, allowing workers to experience low-gravity and sensory-deprived environments safely.

## 5 Conclusions
Extraterrestrial habitats will require robotic processes optimized for planetary environments. The role of human workers will transition from manual labor to managing complex, automated cyber-physical systems.

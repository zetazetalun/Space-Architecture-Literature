# System Architecture and Design Parameters for Extrusion-Based Autonomous Construction Systems\n\n**Authors:** Albert E. Patterson, Bhaskar Vajipeyajula, William R. Norris  \n**Conference:** ASME 2022 International Additive Manufacturing Conference (IAM2022)  \n**Date:** October 19-20, 2022, Lisbon, Portugal\n\n## Abstract\nAutonomous construction systems (ACSs) have become a topic of great interest in areas including space exploration and extraterrestrial habitat construction. This article extracts information about major components, subsystems, and interfaces from published literature to propose a quasi-general system architecture and identify design opportunities for extrusion-based autonomous construction systems (EBACSs).\n\n## 1. Introduction\nEBACSs utilize additive manufacturing principles to extrude concrete, asphalt, or foam-based materials in layers. They are intended for building shells, shelters, bridges, or extraterrestrial habitats. The goal is to apply a systems engineering approach to develop a high-level general architecture to reduce cost and schedule risks.\n\n## 2. Fundamental System Tasks\n1. Collect raw materials and mix appropriately.\n2. Transport materials to a nozzle or applicator.\n3. Selectively place material for walls and geometry.\n4. Adjust or ensure proper placement via guides/trowels.\n5. Monitor and control the process from beginning to completion.\n\n## 3. Subsystem Breakdown\n1. **Subsystem 1: Frame or Support** (Positioners: Gantry, Robotic Arm, Cable-driven, etc.)\n2. **Subsystem 2: Prep Software** (Modeling, slicing, and toolpath generation)\n3. **Subsystem 3: Material Prep** (Mixing and transport)\n4. **Subsystem 4: Extruder** (Nozzle assembly and mount)\n5. **Subsystem 5: Orientation System** (Vital for effective material placement)\n6. **Subsystem 6: Forming/Troweling Tool** (Polishing and controlling material)\n7. **Subsystem 7: Control Software/Firmware**\n8. **Subsystem 8: Control Hardware** (Sensors, cameras, and computers)\n\n## 4. System Interfaces\n- **Mechanical Interfaces:** Direct hardware connections within the system.\n- **Data/Information Interfaces:** Communication between subsystems.\n- **Control Interfaces:** Hardware/software that directly controls position, pattern, and extrusion rate.\n\n## 5. System Design Parameters\nKey parameters identified include: degree of integration (modular vs. fully integrated), elimination of interfaces, extruder design, control hardware, forming/trowel design, process monitoring, dynamic system optimization, improved autonomy, human-system interface, and safety/security systems.

# Adaptive Recycled Plastic Architecture: Vacuum-Sealed Chainmail Structures Through Computational Design

**Authors:** Yi Xu, Farzin Lotfi-Jam, and Mustafa Faruki

**Abstract:** This paper explores the potential of recycled plastics (rPET) as a primary construction material for modular chainmail systems. Using a computational workflow, the study optimizes the design, testing, and fabrication of vacuum-sealed chainmail structures. The research identifies the rectangular configuration as most efficient for architectural use, specifically for extreme conditions including extraterrestrial habitats.

## Introduction
The study focuses on high-performance materials created in laboratory environments, specifically vacuum-sealed chainmail structures that possess variable stiffness, flexibility, and outstanding mechanical properties. It addresses the global plastic waste crisis by integrating rPET filaments into 3D-printed architectural systems.

## Methodology

### Chainmail System Setup
Three geometric configurations were tested (Triangular, Circular, and Rectangular) with a constant solid-to-gap ratio ($R_{sg}$) of 8% and a uniform part diameter of 1mm.

| Unit Type | Formula (Torque/Inertia) | Dimensions (L/b) | Diameter (d) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Triangular | $I = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{36} L^3 d$ | 7.5 mm | 1 mm |
| Circular | $I = \frac{1}{2} R^3 d^2$ | 11 mm | 1 mm |
| Rectangular | $I = \frac{1}{12} (Lb^3 + bL^3) d$ | 11 mm | 1 mm |

### Computational Design Workflow
1.  **2D Sectional Testing:** Evaluation of curvature adaptability.
2.  **3D Shell Structure Generation:** Based on optimal curvature (Amplitude $A$ and Frequency $f$).
3.  **Filtering Process:** Selection of distinct geometries using Perimeter ($P$) and Surface Area ($A$) tolerances.
4.  **Physical Modeling:** 1:20 scaled prototypes in vacuum-sealed environments.
5.  **Load Analysis:** Using Karamba3D for dead, live, snow, and wind loads.

## Findings

### Material Efficiency
Under identical 8% $R_{sg}$ conditions, the rectangular chainmail system exhibited the lowest overall weight (5g) compared to circular (12g) and triangular (14g) systems, making it the most material-efficient choice.

### Structural Performance
- **Stiffness Enhancement:** Vacuum-sealed chainmail systems attain stiffness enhancements of up to 25 times under pressures reaching 2 MPa.
- **Deformation:** The rectangular system showed superior deformation capacity with a maximum amplitude of 35mm at frequency 9.
- **Deflection:** All tested iterations in Groups 1–4 met the deflection criteria (limit of 8mm). The optimized structural configuration resulted in a maximum displacement of 3.56 mm.

## Discussion: Future Applications
- **Space Debris Shielding:** Lightweight, adaptable shield against space debris.
- **Extraterrestrial Habitats:** Lightweight and modular nature is advantageous for spacecraft transport where costs are high.

## Conclusion
The study provides a foundation for using rPET and vacuum-sealed chainmail in extreme environments, bridging waste management with high-performance architectural design.

# Primary Stress Factors and Adaptive Mechanisms of Microalgae in Space Environments and Their Applications in Space Life Support Systems

**Authors:** Siyao Dai, Weiying Feng, Jeffrey Lae, Xuezheng Yu, Chia Min Ho, Fang Yang, Qingfeng Miao, Pengcheng Duan
**Journal:** Plants (MDPI)
**Date:** 2026

## Abstract
Microalgae (cyanobacteria and eukaryotic microalgae) are ideal candidates for bioregenerative life support systems (BLSS) due to high metabolic efficiency and robustness. This review summarizes their responses to space stressors (radiation, vacuum, microgravity) and their applications in atmospheric regulation, food supplementation, and wastewater recycling for planetary bases.

## 1. Introduction
Sustainable life support systems are critical for deep space exploration. Microalgae have high photosynthetic efficiency and resilience, making them leading candidates for closed-loop ecology in off-world colonization. Examples include the MELiSSA system and the Lunar Palace 1.

## 4. Application of Algae in Space Life Support Systems
- **Atmospheric Regulation:** *Chlorella* sp. can achieve peak O2 production of 15 mmol/(L·h).
- **Food Supplementation:** Microalgae provide high protein (50-70% dry weight) and essential fatty acids (EPA/DHA).
- **Water Purification:** *Chlorella* and *Scenedesmus* exhibit high nitrogen and phosphorus removal from wastewater/urine.

## 5. Research Prospects and Challenges
Future research focuses on ISRU (utilizing regolith and lithic matrices) for localized algal cultivation and the development of automated harvesting technologies for planetary bases.

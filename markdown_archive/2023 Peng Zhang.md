# Overview of the Lunar In Situ Resource Utilization Techniques for Future Lunar Missions

**Authors:** Peng Zhang, Wei Dai, Ran Niu, Guang Zhang, Guanghui Liu, Xin Liu, Zheng Bo, Zhi Wang, Haibo Zheng, Chengbao Liu, Hanzhe Yang, Yifan Bai, Yang Zhang, Dong Yan, Kefa Zhou, and Ming Gao
**Journal:** Space: Science & Technology
**Date:** 2023
**DOI:** 10.34133/space.0037

## Abstract
This article provides a comprehensive review of the past developing processes of ISRU and the latest progress of several ISRU technologies, including in situ water access, in situ oxygen production, in situ construction and manufacture, in situ energy utilization, and in situ life support and plant cultivation on the Moon. Ultimately, a 3-step development plan for lunar ISRU technologies in the next decade is proposed.

## Natural Resources on the Moon

### Mineral Resources

## In Situ Lunar Construction Techniques

### Additive Manufacturing (AM) Techniques
- **Contour Crafting:** A gantry-based 3D printing technique using liquid-state binders (e.g., sulfur concrete). NASA explored this for quick infrastructure construction.
- **D-Shape:** Uses liquid ink/binder injected into regolith. Suitable for large-scale protective shells, but problematic in low gravity/vacuum due to fluid behavior.

### Sintering Techniques
- **Microwave Sintering:** Provides high efficiency and even heating. Experiments with Apollo 17 soil showed improved formability and strength.
- **Solar Sintering:** Uses Fresnel lenses to concentrate sunlight. Limit is the depth of the sintering process compared to microwave.

### Extrusion Printing of Lunar Regolith Inks
- **Regolith-Resin Inks:** 3D printing slurry using regolith and photocurable resins. Samples achieved an average compressive strength of 428.1 MPa and flexure strength of 129.5 MPa.

## Key Findings and Roadmaps
- **Prioritization:** Water, oxygen, and food production must take priority over other materials.
- **Water Extraction:** Solar-thermal mining in PSRs and hydrogen reduction of ilmenite are primary methods.
- **Roadmap:** 
  - 2027: Mineral resource exploration and assessment.
  - 2030: In situ experiments on the lunar surface.
  - 2035: Pilot-scale replenishment for medium-term residence.
  - 2040: Large-scale replenishment for long-term lunar/Martian missions.

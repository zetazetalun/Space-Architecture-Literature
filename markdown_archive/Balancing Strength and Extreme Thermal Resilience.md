# Balancing Strength and Extreme Thermal Resilience in Lunar Regolith Composites: The Role of Multi-Walled Carbon Nanotubes

**Authors:** Andrea J. Hoe, Amirreza Tarafdar, Wenhua Lin, Michael R. Fiske, Jennifer E. Edmunson, and Yeqing Wang
**Publication:** Small, 2025
**DOI:** 10.1002/smll.202502220

## Abstract
This study explores the reinforcement of lunar regolith with multi-walled carbon nanotubes (MWCNTs) for building semi-permanent Moon bases. Composites incorporating varying percentages (0.00 to 1.00 wt.%) of MWCNTs were subjected to freeze-thaw cycles, vacuum pressures, and ambient environments to simulate lunar conditions.

## Introduction
Transporting material to orbit costs approximately $10,000 per pound. In situ resource utilization (ISRU) of lunar regolith is essential. This research utilizes lunar regolith simulant (LSP-1), urea (as a superplasticizer from potential human waste), and MWCNTs to create a high-strength composite.

## Experimental Methodology
Three processing routes were evaluated:
1. **Route 1:** Ambient curing without temperature cycling.
2. **Route 2:** Ambient curing with four thermal cycles (+117 °C to -80 °C).
3. **Route 3:** Vacuum curing followed by thermal cycling.

### Material Composition
- **Lunar Regolith Simulant:** ~77.36 wt.% (80:20 fine-to-coarse ratio).
- **Acidic Solution:** ~19.50 wt.% (Phosphoric acid and water).
- **MWCNTs:** 0.00, 0.25, 0.50, and 1.00 wt.%.
- **Urea:** Used as a superplasticizer to reduce water content to 12.48 wt.%.

## Results and Discussion

### Mechanical Strength (Route 1)
- **0.00 wt.% MWCNT:** 27 MPa compressive strength.
- **0.50 wt.% MWCNT:** 39 MPa (44.44% increase).
- **1.00 wt.% MWCNT:** 38 MPa (Slight decrease due to poor interfacial bonding).

### Thermal Cycling Effects (Routes 2 & 3)
- **Route 2 (Ambient + Cycling):** Strength dropped significantly below 20 MPa.
- **Route 3 (Vacuum + Cycling):** MWCNT-free samples achieved 47 MPa. However, samples with MWCNTs performed poorly (e.g., 1.00 wt.% reached only 28 MPa).

### Key Mechanisms
- **CTE Mismatch:** MWCNTs have a negative coefficient of thermal expansion (CTE) of -1.2 × 10⁻⁵/°C, while regolith components (SiO2, Al2O3, CaO) have positive CTEs. This mismatch causes microcracking and delamination during temperature swings.
- **Particle Size Tuning:** An 80:20 fine-to-coarse ratio optimized packing density and reduced voids.
- **Vacuum Curing:** Promotes densification and controlled evaporation, leading to higher strengths in unreinforced samples.

## Conclusion
MWCNTs significantly enhance compressive strength under stable temperatures (ambient). However, thermal mismatch between MWCNTs and the regolith matrix reduces performance during lunar-representative thermal cycling. Improving CTE compatibility is critical for future extraterrestrial construction materials.

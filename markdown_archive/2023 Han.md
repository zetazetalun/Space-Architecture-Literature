# Structural synthesis of the reconfigurable legged mobile lander for strong geometry-physical interaction with extraterrestrial environment

**Authors:** Youcheng Han, Weizhong Guo, Changjie Zhao, Ziyue Li, Ze Fu, Yinghui Li  
**Journal:** Mechanism and Machine Theory 189 (2023) 105446  
**Published:** July 2023  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mechmachtheory.2023.105446

## Abstract
To design the engineering prototype of reconfigurable legged mobile lander (ReLML) for strong geometry-physical interaction with extraterrestrial environment, this paper proposes a tailored structural synthesis methodology unifying kinematic geometry and statics. Compared with preceding works, three inbuilt operation modes are first integrated: **adjusting mode** (3R) with strong geometrical adaptation to hostile terrain; **landing mode** (2R1T) inherited from Chang’E lander for impact survival; and **roving mode** (2R1T) for motion/force interactivity. The ReLML can serve the alien base construction and adjustable launch bracket. The unified structural synthesis methodology of kinematic geometry and statics is universal and effective for designing parallel mechanisms and reconfigurable mechanisms.

## 1. Introduction
Extraterrestrial exploration is evolving toward innovative intelligent detection equipment. Current lander types include cantilever-type and tripod-type legged stationary landers. The paper identifies five development routes, focusing on the fifth: integrating both legged passive buffering and legged locomotion (the legged mobile lander) to build human and/or robotized lunar bases. 

## 2. Methodology
The tailored mission-oriented structural synthesis methodology involves:
1.  **Defining requirements:** Identification of functional modes (adjusting, landing, roving).
2.  **Extracting characteristics:** Analyzing energy transmission and force/motion interactions with the environment.
3.  **Deriving output finite motion:** Arranging Chasles' axes for optimal work efficiency.
4.  **Deriving instantaneous force characteristics:** Balancing interaction, transmission, and constraint force systems.
5.  **Metamorphic synthesis:** Designing limbs and joints that switch functions across modes.

## 3. Findings & Discussion
- **Adjusting Mode (3R):** Enables large orientation adjustment to match uneven terrain for better touchdown mechanics.
- **Landing Mode (2R1T):** Redefined to survive strong impact interactions through truss-mechanism transformation and buffering.
- **Roving Mode (2R1T):** Optimized for force/motion interactivity during surface locomotion.
- **Synthesis Families:** The paper classifies metamorphic limb bonds into four families based on their degrees of freedom (DoF) and constraint/transmission wrench systems.

## 10. Conclusions
1. Mission-oriented design demands are met by considering strong geometry-physical interactions with the extraterrestrial environment.
2. Three distinct operation modes (adjusting, landing, roving) are integrated into a single compact robotic platform.
3. The methodology uses finite and instantaneous screw theory to extract interaction characteristics and implement motion/force synergistic synthesis.
4. ReLML serves multi-functional roles in extraterrestrial logistics, base construction, and sample launch/return.

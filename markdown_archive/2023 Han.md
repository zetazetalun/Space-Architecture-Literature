# Structural synthesis of the reconfigurable legged mobile lander for strong geometry-physical interaction with extraterrestrial environment

**Journal:** Mechanism and Machine Theory 189 (2023) 105446  
**Authors:** Youcheng Han, Weizhong Guo, Changjie Zhao, Ziyue Li, Ze Fu, Yinghui Li  
**Publisher:** Elsevier Ltd.

## Abstract
To design the engineering prototype of reconfigurable legged mobile lander (ReLML) for strong geometry-physical interaction with extraterrestrial environment, this paper proposes a tailored structural synthesis methodology unifying kinematic geometry and statics. Compared with preceding works, three inbuilt operation modes are integrated: **adjusting mode** (3R rotations for terrain adaptation), **landing mode** (2R1T motion for impact survival), and **roving mode** (2R1T motion for mobility). The methodology allows for the design of mechanical generators and metamorphic limbs that can serve as an alien base construction platform and adjustable launch bracket.

## 1. Introduction
Extraterrestrial exploration requires innovative detection equipment capable of surviving hostile terrains. The paper identifies five development routes for landers, concluding with the proposed ReLML which integrates passive buffering and legged locomotion to support manned or robotized lunar bases. 

## 2. Methodology: Structural Synthesis
The tailored methodology follows these steps:
1.  **Define Requirements:** Identify functional operation modes (Adjusting, Landing, Roving).
2.  **Extract Interaction Characteristics:** Establish energy transmission and conversion equations and force characteristics.
3.  **Derive Output Finite Motion:** Determine Chasles’ axes for end-effectors in multi-modes.
4.  **Derive Instantaneous Force Characteristics:** Balance interaction, transmission, and constraint forces.
5.  **Unified Topology Synthesis:** Synthesize metamorphic limbs and joints to assemble the execution mechanism.

## 3. Limb and Joint Design
The paper categorizes metamorphic limbs into four families based on their degrees of freedom (DoF) and constraint/transmission requirements. 
- **Family 1:** dim{mSf,L,j} = 3 (3 DoFs in each mode).
- **Family 2:** dim{mSf,L,j} = 4.
- **Family 3:** dim{mSf,L,j} = 5.
- **Family 4:** dim{mSf,L,j} = 6.

Typical mechanical generators are illustrated for these families, integrating active metamorphic joints (mRv) and passive metamorphic buffering dampers (mPb).

## 4. Assembly and Evolution
The ReLML execution mechanism is assembled based on subcategories of limb layouts. 
- **Subcategory 1:** Symmetric topologies for 5-DoF limbs.
- **Subcategory 2:** Hybrid limbs with 4-, 5-, and 6-DoF bonds.
- **Subcategory 3:** Simplest assembly condition, utilizing two common finite rotation axes across modes.

## 10. Conclusions
- Proposed a tailored synthesis methodology for ReLML prototypes.
- Mission-oriented demands are met through integrated adjusting (3R), landing (2R1T), and roving (2R1T) modes.
- The ReLML serves as a versatile platform for alien base construction, orientation adjustment, and terrain adaptation.

# Structural synthesis of the reconfigurable legged mobile lander for strong geometry-physical interaction with extraterrestrial environment\n\n**Authors:** Youcheng Han, Weizhong Guo, Changjie Zhao, Ziyue Li, Ze Fu, Yinghui Li\n**Journal:** Mechanism and Machine Theory 189 (2023) 105446\n**Publisher:** Elsevier Ltd.\n\n## ABSTRACT\nTo design the engineering prototype of reconfigurable legged mobile lander (ReLML) for strong geometry-physical interaction with extraterrestrial environment, this paper proposes a tailored structural synthesis methodology unifying kinematic geometry and statics. Compared with our preceding works, three inbuilt operation modes are first integrated: adjusting mode is a totally new mode defined as three rotations (3R) with a strong geometrical adaptation to hostile terrain; landing mode is redefined as two rotations one translation (2R1T), inherits Chang'E lander to survive from strong impact interaction; roving mode turns into the other 2R1T motion for good motion/force interactivity. The interaction characteristics (motion, force, energy) between ReLML and environment are extracted to derive output finite motions and instantaneous wrenches of leg end-effector, following the principles of optimal force system equilibrium and work efficiency. The motion and force synergistic syntheses of metamorphic limb and joint are implemented to design and assemble mechanical generators. The ReLML can serve the alien base construction and adjustable launch bracket. The unified structural synthesis methodology of kinematic geometry and statics is universal and effective for designing parallel mechanisms and reconfigurable mechanisms, especially for strong physical interaction scenarios.\n\n## 1. Introduction\nAs many countries successfully launched the cantilever-type and tripod-type legged stationary landers to the moon and Mars, the wave of extraterrestrial exploration has gradually developed... Consequently, there exist key scientific issues to create the novel detection probes, namely, how to integrate the buffer principle research for heavy-load landing into the design stage, how to endow the landing leg with adjustable touchdown foothold and walking ability, how to endow the overall lander with orientation adjustment ability to serve the lunar base construction and the adjustable launch bracket for sample return.\n\n## 3. Procedure of the structural synthesis methodology for strong ReLML-environment interaction in three operation modes from adjusting, landing, to roving\nTill now, the soft-landing detection probes have been limited to flat and friendly extraterrestrial areas, but still cannot visit hostile terrain... The strong ReLML-environment interaction demands there must unify both kinematic geometry and statics into the structural synthesis process.\n\n## 4. Extract the interaction characteristics for strong ReLML-environment interaction in three modes\nConsidering the equivalent topology model constructed by ReLML and environment, the ReLML has two kinds of end-effectors, i.e., the footpad and the main body. The input, output, and interaction characteristics in aspects of motion, force, and energy for strong ReLML-environment interaction are depicted.\n\n## 10. Conclusions\nThis paper proposes a tailored structural synthesis methodology for the ReLML engineering prototype. The main innovation points and contributions are:\n(1) The mission-oriented design demands are guaranteed: the strong geometry-physical interaction between ReLML and extraterrestrial environment is fully considered, the compact and feasible topologies with fewer limbs and joints are synthesized for reliable reconfigurability;\n(2) The legged mobile lander is first defined and integrated with three operation modes exactly: adjusting mode is a totally new mode with 3R to adapt to hostile terrain geometry; landing mode is redefined as 2R1T; roving mode turns into a new 2R1T motion.\n(3) The structural synthesis methodology for ReLML unifying kinematic geometry and statics is proposed.\n(4) The metamorphosis principle is demonstrated for ReLML to reveal the reconfiguration essence of topology.\n(5) The ReLML can serve the alien base construction and adjustable bracket for sample launch and return, owing to the multiple functional integrations of fold and deployment, foothold adjustment, landing buffering, roving walking, orientation adjustment, terrain adaptation, etc.

# Concept for the Future Utilization of Lunar Underground Space and Adaptive Design Strategies

**Authors:** Xing Xu, Minghui Xue
**Journal:** Buildings 2025, 15, 4057
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.3390/buildings15224057

## Abstract
This paper reviews the historical development and current research on lunar underground space utilization. It proposes five guiding principles for progressive exploration and construction and presents a phased “1.0–4.0 era” framework. Adaptive design strategies (spatial, environmental, and climatic) are proposed to guide future lunar habitation and ensure sustainable development.

## 1. Introduction
The Moon is a primary target for future development. Research has shifted from surface facilities to underground spaces like lava tubes due to extreme surface conditions (radiation, temperature fluctuations, micrometeorites). 

## 2. Advantages of Lunar Underground Space
- **Thermal Stability:** A constant-temperature layer exists ~1 m below the regolith at ~250 K (-20 °C).
- **Radiation Shielding:** At a depth of 6 m, radiation can be completely shielded. Even 1-2 m of weathered layer in lava tube walls provides significant protection.
- **Scale:** Lunar lava tubes are vast, with diameters ranging from meters to over a kilometer.

## 3. Phased Development Framework (1.0–4.0 Eras)
- **Stage 1.0 (Point-Based):** Initial small-scale facilities (communication hubs, energy stations) focused on scientific exploration.
- **Stage 2.0 (Linear-Based):** Connecting facilities via transportation corridors and energy pipelines within lava tubes.
- **Stage 3.0 (Areal-Based):** Interconnected point and linear spaces forming multifunctional complexes within the thermal stable layer.
- **Stage 4.0 (Three-Dimensional Underground Ecological City):** Large-scale networked urban networks with integrated life support, ecological vegetation, and resource recycling.

## 5. Adaptive Design Strategies
- **Spatial Adaptability:** Modularization of facilities, structural reliability, and use of natural geological features.
- **Environmental Adaptability:** Use of high-efficiency insulating regolith and construction of closed-loop ecological systems.
- **Climatic Adaptability:** Passive energy-saving design, diverse ecological landscapes, and renewable energy utilization (solar, solar wind, cosmic rays).

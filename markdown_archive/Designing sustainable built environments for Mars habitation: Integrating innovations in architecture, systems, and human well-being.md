# Designing sustainable built environments for Mars habitation

## Abstract Summary
This review provides a multidisciplinary perspective on developing Mars habitation modules, moving from initial design to operational efficiency. It emphasizes the integration of resource sustainability (using in situ resources) with human-centered environmental control to support long-term Martian migration.

## Comparison of Environments
| Feature | Earth | ISS | Moon | Mars |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gravity** | 1 g | None | 1/6 g | 1/3 g |
| **Atmosphere** | 78% N2, 21% O2 | Artificial | None | Rarefied CO2 (95.3%) |
| **Pressure** | 101.3 kPa | None | 3 x 10^-13 kPa | 0.6 kPa |
| **In situ resources** | Wide varieties | None | SiO2, Al2O3, CaO | Martian soil, Basalt, Volcanic ash |

## Habitat Construction and Materials
### ISRU and 3D Printing
- **Martian Basalt:** Abundant surface material with radiation resistance and high specific heat.
- **Sulfur-based Concrete:** Suitable for Mars due to ease of operation and fast solidification in cold environments. It achieves a compressive strength of approximately 4.62 kgf/mm².
- **3D Printing:** Technologies such as internal printing chambers (e.g., Team Zopherus) and robotic assembly systems (e.g., Chinese Super Mason) are highlighted for autonomous construction.
- **Habitat Classes:**
  - **Above-ground:** Pre-fabricated or inflatable cores with outer shells (e.g., Mars Ice House, Marsha).
  - **Underground/Semi-underground:** Utilizing Martian lava tubes to protect against radiation and thermal extremes.

## Environmental Control Systems (ECS)
- **Thermal Management:** Use of heat pipes, aerogels (thermal conductivity 0.031–0.038 W/(m·K)), and phase change materials.
- **Air & Water:** Regenerative life support systems, CO2 conversion to oxygen via solid oxide electrolysis, and water extraction from subsurface ice (e.g., RedWater system).
- **Energy:** Integration of BIPV (Building Integrated Photovoltaics), flexible solar panels, and potential nuclear energy sources.

## Human Factors
- **Bionic Environments:** Implementation of rhythmic artificial lighting to maintain circadian rhythms and dynamic indoor temperatures to improve thermal adaptability.
- **Psychological Well-being:** Private spaces and movable partitions to combat isolation and occlusion.

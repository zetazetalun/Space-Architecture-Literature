# Wettability characteristics of lunar soil particles from the Chang’E-5 mission: A single-particle surface analysis

**Journal:** Applied Surface Science 702 (2025) 163331  
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.apsusc.2025.163331](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apsusc.2025.163331)  
**Authors:** Haotian Zhang, Jun Liu, Zhiping Yuan, Zhenxing Li, Xiaoyu Yao, Qianghui Xu, Tongcai Wang, Yanjie Zheng, Gong Wang, Hu Zhang, Jun Shen

## Abstract
Lunar base construction has emerged as a strategic focus for global space agencies, with extrusion-based in situ resource utilization (ISRU) technologies recognized as a promising approach. A critical aspect of this technology lies in understanding the wettability characteristics between lunar soil and binding agents, such as water or resin. This study quantitatively analyzed wetting dynamics through a custom-developed experimental apparatus combining ultra-deep field microscopy with a precision liquid evaporator. Results indicate that the contact angle between a particle and water averages 108.73° (hydrophobic), attributed to surface morphology (adherent structures, pores, mineral fragments). Numerical simulations confirm the Cassie-Baxter model accurately describes the observed phenomena. A surface tension of 8.46 mN/m was determined for the particles.

## 1. Introduction
Lunar soil, an abundant raw material, is well-suited for additive manufacturing technologies including stereolithography (SLA), selective laser sintering (SLS), and directed energy deposition (DED). Extrusion molding (ED) offers advantages of rapid forming and simplicity. However, the compatibility between lunar soil and organic additives like resins is a key challenge.

## 2. Experimental
### 2.1. Lunar soil pretreatment
Sample code CE5C0400 (500 mg) from Chang’E-5 mission (Oceanus Procellarum) was used. Particles (~hundreds of micrometers) were selected.
### 2.2. Characterization
FESEM and EDS were used for surface morphology and composition (Oxygen, Silicon, Iron, etc.).
### 2.3. Contact angle measurement
A custom system using an ultra-deep field microscope (UDFM) and a liquid evaporator (LE) was used to measure contact angles on micron-sized particles.

## 3. Results and Discussion
- **Surface Morphology:** Particles exhibit angular shapes with glassy condensates, porous structures, and mineral fragments due to space weathering.
- **Wettability with Water:** Average contact angle (θ) was 108.73° ± 3.60°, indicating hydrophobicity, unlike terrestrial pyroxene/olivine which are typically hydrophilic.
- **Numerical Simulation:** Two-phase flow phase-field method in COMSOL Multiphysics was used. Results follow the Cassie-Baxter model.
- **Surface Tension:** Estimated at 8.46 mN/m.
- **Interaction with Resin:** Calculated Young contact angle for resin-particle interaction was 96.18° (theoretical) vs 91.90° (Young equation).

## 4. Conclusions
The study establishes a framework for characterizing contact angles on micron-scale rough particles, providing empirical support for extrusion-based extraterrestrial construction using resin binders.

# Mechanical and thermal properties of pressureless sintered lunar regolith simulant: Analysis of sintering parameters and pore structure

## Abstract
Sintering has emerged as one of the most promising techniques for in-situ resource utilization in lunar construction. Pressureless sintering, owing to its process simplicity and low equipment requirements, offers a feasible route for fabricating large and complex structural components. This study systematically investigates the effects of sintering temperature, holding time, and atmosphere on the mechanical properties, pore structure, thermal behavior, phase evolution, and microstructure of pressureless-sintered compacts using CUG-1B lunar regolith simulant. Results indicate that compacts sintered in air exhibit superior mechanical strength and reduced open porosity. Sintering temperature exerted a more significant influence on compact properties than holding time. Furthermore, sintering kinetics and pore-property relationships were explored, revealing that open pores (rather than isolated pores) predominantly degrade mechanical performance. In contrast, isolated pores significantly enhance thermal insulation.

## 1. Introduction
Lunar development requires the use of abundant local materials, particularly lunar regolith, as construction resources through in-situ resource utilization (ISRU). Sintering has attracted significant attention due to its procedural simplicity and high utilization efficiency.

### 2.2. Sample preparation and sintering
CUG-1B LRS powder was pressed at 100 MPa. Sintering temperatures were 1125 °C, 1150 °C, and 1175 °C with holding times of 60, 180, and 300 minutes in air and argon atmospheres.

## 5. Conclusions
(1) The oxygen-free environment (argon) exerts a detrimental effect on the mechanical properties compared to air-sintered compacts. (2) Open porosity is the primary factor degrading mechanical properties, while isolated porosity can optimize thermal insulation. (3) Sintered compacts with performance above 20 MPa meet the requirements for most lunar construction applications.

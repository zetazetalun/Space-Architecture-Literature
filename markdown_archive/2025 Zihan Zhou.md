# Dynamic mechanical properties of lunar regolith based geopolymer: Coupled effects of composition and temperature

**Authors:** Zihan Zhou, Xiyao Zheng, Jun Wu
**Journal:** Case Studies in Construction Materials 23 (2025) e05417
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cscm.2025.e05417

## Abstract
This study developed a high-fidelity lunar regolith simulant (GCD-2) based on Chang’e-6 samples and synthesized a 'one-part' lunar regolith-based geopolymer (LRG). The dynamic mechanical properties were evaluated using the Split Hopkinson Pressure Bar (SHPB). Investigations focused on precursor Ca/Si and Al/Si ratios and lunar thermal cycling. Results showed that a Ca/Si ratio of 0.35 and Al/Si of 0.50 optimized performance, yielding a peak dynamic compressive strength of 70.77 MPa. The study identifies an optimal curing window of 100 hours prior to the lunar daytime high-temperature phase.

## 1. Introduction
Establishing a permanent lunar base is a priority for international space exploration (NASA's Artemis, CNSA's lunar base by 2030). The high cost of Earth-to-Moon transport necessitates In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU), specifically using lunar regolith. Geopolymers are promising candidates for lunar construction due to their rapid setting and high strength. This paper addresses the lack of data on dynamic mechanical properties (impact resistance) of LRGs and the influence of lunar thermal cycles.

## 2. Materials and Methods
### 2.1 Materials
- **GCD-2 Simulant:** Replicates CE-6 geochemical and mineralogical characteristics (Plagioclase, pyroxene, olivine).
- **Activator:** Solid sodium silicate (SS) granules.
- **Preparation:** 'One-part' method (dry mixing simulant and activator, then adding water).

### 2.2 Curing Conditions
Two batches were tested to simulate lunar surface conditions at 30° latitude:
- **Batch 1:** Curing starts at the onset of the lunar daytime heating phase (73°C → 113°C → -178°C).
- **Batch 2:** Curing starts during the cooling phase (73°C → -178°C → 113°C).

## 3. Results and Discussion
### 3.1 Dynamic Mechanical Properties
- **Batch 1** showed significantly higher stiffness (avg. modulus 1.78 GPa) compared to **Batch 2** (avg. 1.35 GPa).
- **Ca/Si Ratio:** Increasing the ratio to 0.35 enhanced geopolymerization, densified the matrix, and improved compressive strength.
- **Al/Si Ratio:** An optimal ratio of 0.50 (Group A2) was found; excessive Al hindered kinetics.
- **Peak Strength:** The C3A3 group (highest Ca/Si and high Al/Si) achieved **70.77 MPa**.

### 3.2 Microstructure
- SEM analysis confirmed that Ca-rich systems displayed pronounced densification and reduced microcrack density.
- XRD showed a broad diffuse halo (20–40°), indicating the formation of amorphous geopolymer gels.

## 4. Conclusions
1. High-temperature phases of the lunar day significantly accelerate geopolymerization.
2. Optimal mixing/casting window: within 100 h prior to high lunar temperatures.
3. Precursor Ca/Si ratios near 0.35 maximize impact resistance.
4. Synergistic Ca-Al optimization is critical for structural integrity in extreme environments.

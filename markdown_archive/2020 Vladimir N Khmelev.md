# Ultrasonic tool for the realization of combined action during the drilling of extraterrestrial objects

**Authors:** Vladimir N Khmelev, Roman V Barsukov, Viktor A Nesterov, Dmitry V Genne, Qiquan Quan, and Roman N Golykh
**Published:** 2020, Journal of Physics: Conference Series 1679 (2020) 042033
**DOI:** 10.1088/1742-6596/1679/4/042033

## Abstract
The article is devoted to the problem of the efficiency increase of ultrasonic drilling of hard materials for the studies of the soils of the planets, asteroids and other space objects. It is proposed the solution of the problem by the help of developed device, which is able to realize simultaneously with ultrasound the action by low-frequency macro-oscillations and rotational motion of the leading edge of the tool. The tests of the practical construction of the developed device showed the possibility of the speed increase of the ultrasonic drilling due to the additional low-frequency oscillations and pseudo-rotation in up to 1.5 times.

## 1. Introduction
Large and small extraterrestrial objects, such as planets, comets, asteroids are the objects for studies. Soil intake is usually carried out in the form of cores taken by drilling hollow tubular tools. Traditional drilling methods (by rotated drill) are not practical in low gravitation due to high clamping forces required and heat generation which can volatilize water or low-melting materials. Ultrasonic drilling is considered an efficient alternative as it requires less pressing force and avoids heating the drilling zone.

## 2. Construction Description
The proposed device combines:
- **Ultrasonic frequency oscillations:** ~20 kHz with an amplitude of ~70 µm.
- **Low-frequency macro-oscillations:** Provided by a shock periodic action of a loading mass (13), with amplitudes up to ten times greater than the ultrasonic action.
- **Pseudo-rotational motion:** Provides periodic motion of the construction to increase cutting productivity.

## 3. Results of the Experiments
Experiments were conducted using silicate brick, red construction brick, and foam concrete. Operation modes tested included:
- Rotation only (RD)
- Ultrasound only (UD)
- Ultrasound + Low-frequency vibrations (UD+LFV)
- Ultrasound + Rotation (UD+RD)
- Ultrasound + Low-frequency vibrations + Rotation (UD+LFV+RD)

## 4. Conclusion
The implementation of complex action (ultrasound + LFV + rotation) provided a growth in drilling speed by up to 49% compared to basic ultrasonic drilling. The device excludes energy losses from tool separation and allows for continuous control of electric parameters to define soil characteristics during the process.

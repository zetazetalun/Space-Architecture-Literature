# Ultrasonic tool for the realization of combined action during the drilling of extraterrestrial objects

**Authors:** Vladimir N Khmelev, Roman V Barsukov, Viktor A Nesterov, Dmitry V Genne, Qiquan Quan and Roman N Golykh
**Journal:** Journal of Physics: Conference Series, 1679 (2020) 042033
**DOI:** 10.1088/1742-6596/1679/4/042033

## Abstract
The article is devoted to the problem of the efficiency increase of ultrasonic drilling of hard materials for the studies of the soils of the planets, asteroids and other space objects. It is proposed the solution of the problem by the help of developed device, which is able to realize simultaneously with ultrasound the action by low-frequency macro-oscillations and rotational motion of the leading edge of the tool. The tests of the practical construction of the developed device showed the possibility of the speed increase of the ultrasonic drilling due to the additional low-frequency oscillations and pseudo-rotation in up to 1.5 times.

## 1. Introduction
Drilling on extraterrestrial objects (planets, comets, asteroids) is essential for chemical analysis and finding life forms. On small bodies with low gravitation, traditional rotary drilling is impractical due to the high clamping force required and excessive heat generation, which can volatilize water and low-melting materials. Ultrasonic drilling is proposed as an alternative as it requires low pressing force, excludes heating, and can handle various materials.

## 2. Construction Description
The developed device combines ultrasonic drilling with shock low-frequency oscillations and rotational motion. The tool consists of a piezoelectric transducer (20 kHz), a concentrator, and a tubular working tool. Low-frequency oscillations (approx. 20 Hz) are generated by a loading mass that rebounds from the oscillating part of the concentrator. A pseudo-rotational drive provides periodic motion to the entire construction.

## 4. Conclusion
The study proved that combined action (UD+LFV+RD) increases drilling speed by approximately 49% (1.5 times) compared to pure ultrasonic drilling and 3-10 times compared to traditional rotation. The system allows for continuous control of electric parameters to define soil characteristics during drilling without separating the tool from the oscillating system.

# Overall Material Flow Scheme Design of Controlled Ecological Life Support System for Lunar Base

**Authors:** Yibing Deng, Liangchang Zhang, Qiang Bian, Weidang Ai, Yongkang Tang, Ruixin Mao, and Haonan Fan
**Journal:** Space: Science & Technology (A Science Partner Journal)
**DOI:** 10.34133/space.0236

## Abstract
The lunar base is likely to be the next long-term extraterrestrial base for humans. The cost of lunar replenishment necessitates living systems with high material closure. This paper proposes a 4-stage introduction of biological components (plants, microalgae, and microorganisms) to establish an upgraded Controlled Ecological Life Support System (CELSS) with a total material closure degree of 98%.

## Introduction
Long-term human habitation on the lunar surface (e.g., Artemis, Moon Village, ILRS) requires advanced life support. CELSS provides circular supply of oxygen, water, and food through biological regeneration, reducing dependence on terrestrial resupply.

## Methods
The study designs a matching CELSS for different stages of lunar exploration. The system integrates "producer-consumer-decomposer" ecological principles into a closed environment. 

### Functional Configuration of CELSS Subsystems (Table 1 Summary)
| ID | Subsystem | Function |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | CO2 Removal | Adsorption/Absorption (early stage) |
| 2 | O2 Generation | H2O Electrolysis (early stage) |
| 3 | CO2 Reduction | Sabatier/Chemical catalytic process |
| 4 | Urine Processing | Distillation-based water recovery |
| 5 | Water Recovery | Deep purification (catalytic oxidation) |
| 6 | Food–Vegetable Production | Plant photosynthesis (fresh veg) |
| 7 | Food–Grain Production | Staple food production (later stages) |
| 8 | Waste Dewatering | Liquid recovery from solid waste |
| 9 | Wastewater Bioreactor | Microbial pretreatment of urine/washwater |
| 10 | Solid Waste Bioreactor | Fermentation of biomass/feces into fertilizer |
| 11 | Brine/Feces Processing | 98% water recovery from urine/feces |
| 12 | Microalgae Reactor | O2 regeneration and algal protein food |

## Results

### Four-Stage Application Strategy
1.  **Stage I — Prophase Lunar Base (20 weeks - 20 months):** Physicochemical dominant; small-scale vegetable production (~1 m²); 83.4% total closure.
2.  **Stage II — Preliminary Lunar Base (2-5 years):** Microalgae reactor added; wastewater bioreactors; 95.2% total closure.
3.  **Stage III — Medium Lunar Base (5-10 years):** Grain production introduced (~17.55 m² per person); 96.9% total closure.
4.  **Stage IV — Upgrade Lunar Base (>10 years):** Large-scale production (~25.68 m² total); solid waste bioreactors; 98% total closure.

## Discussion
- **Water Recovery:** Essential for closure. Non-oxidizing technical means (anaerobic fermentation) recover water from feces.
- **Application Sequence:** Fresh vegetables take precedence over grain due to lower energy demand and psychological benefits.
- **ISRU Potential:** Microalgae can utilize in situ resources like lunar ice (water) and CO2 from systems, and could potentially assist in lunar soil amelioration.

## Conclusion
The study provides a reference for the phased construction of lunar bases at minimum cost through modular, scalable biological integration. Future research must verify these systems in actual space environments (ISS/CSS).

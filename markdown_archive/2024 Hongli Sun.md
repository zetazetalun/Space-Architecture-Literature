# Designing sustainable built environments for Mars habitation: Integrating innovations in architecture, systems, and human well-being

**Authors:** Hongli Sun, Mengfan Duan, Yifan Wu, Yunyi Zeng, Hengxin Zhao, Shuangdui Wu, Borong Lin, Ronggui Yang, and Gang Tan

**Journal:** Nexus 1, 100030, September 17, 2024
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.ynexs.2024.100030](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ynexs.2024.100030)

## Summary
Migration to Mars and building livable habitation gradually become prosperous and arduous roads for human progress. Although many technical breakthroughs have been achieved for supporting migration, sustainable survival on Mars needs further attention. This review provides a systemic outlook of promising Mars habitation environment control technologies for the sustainability of both habitation resources and the occupant’s health.

## Introduction
Mars dwelling involve two key aspects: resource sustainability and human sustainability, encompassing both the efficient construction and operation of Mars habitation and the healthy survival of humans. In situ resources on Mars are utilized for module construction through the novel 3D printing technology, which is also expected to protect humans from space radiation risk and heavy ions.

## Mars Habitation Technologies

### Design of Mars Habitation Module
- **Above-ground:** Prefab pressurized (often inflatable) cores with shells (e.g., Mars Ice House, Marsha).
- **Underground/Semi-underground:** Utilizing Martian lava tubes to withstand harsh space risks.
- **Materials:** 
  - **Basalt:** Abundant source for radiation resistance and low permeability.
  - **Martian Soil/Regolith:** Mixed with elemental sulfur to create **sulfur-based concrete**. 
  - **Sulfur Concrete Properties:** Ease of operation, fast solidification, recyclability, and a compressive strength of approximately 4.62 kgf/mm² on Mars.

### Construction Methods
- **3D Printing:** Offers possibilities for using in situ materials.
- **Robotic Construction:** Automated systems for assembling modules (e.g., Team Zopherus, Chinese Super Mason).

### Environmental Control and Operation
- **Thermal Control:** High-insulation materials, heat pipes, and phase change materials.
- **Air Circulation:** Integrated systems for separation, filtration, and regeneration.
- **Lighting:** Rhythmic artificial lighting to support circadian cycles in the absence of natural daylight cycles.
- **Energy:** PV panel efficiency on Mars ranges between 8% and 13.5%, with specific power of 1–2 kW/kg.

## Challenges and Future Opportunities
1. Module form and architectural layout.
2. Construction methods and automation.
3. Material selection for in situ construction.
4. Sustainable energy sources (Solar, Nuclear, Wind).
5. Human health and psychological comfort in isolation.

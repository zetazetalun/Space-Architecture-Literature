# Lunar construction: A state-of-the-art survey

**Authors:** Ketan Vasudeva, M. Reza Emami, Cameron Dickinson  
**Journal:** Progress in Aerospace Sciences 161 (2026) 101159  
**Published:** 2026 (Online Dec 2025)  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.paerosci.2025.101159

## Abstract
The renewed global interest in establishing a sustainable lunar base has catalyzed efforts to develop robust and scalable lunar construction technologies. This survey presents a comprehensive review of environmental characterization, infrastructure development, construction methods, materials, and robotic systems. It highlights unique challenges like extreme temperature variations, radiation, and abrasive lunar dust.

## 1. Lunar Environment & Regolith
*   **Gravity:** ~1/6 g (1.63 m/s²).
*   **Atmosphere:** Negligible (3 × 10⁻¹⁵ atm).
*   **Temperature:** -171 °C to 111 °C at Apollo sites; below -231 °C in PSRs (Permanently Shadowed Regions).
*   **Radiation Shielding:** 1 m of regolith shielding is generally sufficient for nominal conditions; 2–3 m provides 40 g/cm² of shielding.
*   **Regolith Geotechnical Properties:** Bulk density 1.3–1.92 g/cm³, Porosity 40-65%, Cohesion 0.1–3.4 kPa.

## 2. Lunar Infrastructure Categories
| Category | Types | Examples/Technologies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Habitats** | Class I, II, III | Landed (Habot), Inflatable (Pneumoplanet), ISRU-based (ICON Olympus) |
| **Power** | Solar, Nuclear | Photovoltaic arrays, KRUSTY (kilopower) nuclear reactors |
| **Logistics** | Landing Pads, Roads | Blast berms, sintered pavers, sintered roadways (PAVER project) |

## 3. Lunar Construction Methods and Materials
*   **Reaction Solidification:** Dry-Mix/Steam-Injection (DMSI), Sorel Cement, Geopolymer Cement.
*   **Bonding Solidification:** Polymer Cement (epoxy binders), Sulfur Cement (melting sulfur), Biomaterial Cement.
*   **Sintering/Melting:** Laser Melting, Solar Melting, Microwave Sintering, Direct Sintering.
*   **Confinement Formation:** Regolith Bagging (sandbags/RCUs).

## 4. Lunar Robotic Construction Systems
*   **Excavators:** RASSOR (Regolith Advanced Surface Systems Operations Robot), IPEx, LUVMI-X.
*   **Cranes:** LSMS (Lunar Surface Manipulation System), COLDArm (Cold Operable Lunar Deployable Arm).
*   **Mobile Manipulators:** ATHLETE (All-Terrain Hex-Limbed Extra-Terrestrial Explorer), Helelani.
*   **3D Printers:** Contour Crafting, D-Shape, ICON Extruder, MOONRISE payload.

## 5. Summary Analysis
*   **Sintering/Melting** has dominated research over the last 15 years (41.6% of recent works).
*   **Geopolymer Cements** are emerging to address shortcomings of sulfur cements (low melting point).
*   **Robotic teams** are increasingly focused on collaborative tasks like solar panel assembly and tower outfitting (PRO-ACT).

# Ultrasonic tool for the realization of combined action during the drilling of extraterrestrial objects

**Authors:** Vladimir N Khmelev, Roman V Barsukov, Viktor A Nesterov, Dmitry V Genne, Qiquan Quan, and Roman N Golykh  
**Journal:** Journal of Physics: Conference Series  
**Volume/Issue:** 1679 (2020) 042033  
**DOI:** 10.1088/1742-6596/1679/4/042033  

## Abstract
The article is devoted to the problem of the efficiency increase of ultrasonic drilling of hard materials for the studies of the soils of the planets, asteroids and other space objects. It is proposed the solution of the problem by the help of developed device, which is able to realize simultaneously with ultrasound the action by low-frequency macro-oscillations and rotational motion of the leading edge of the tool. The tests of the practical construction of the developed device showed the possibility of the speed increase of the ultrasonic drilling due to the additional low-frequency oscillations and pseudo-rotation in up to 1.5 times.

## 1. Introduction
Drilling extraterrestrial objects is necessary for determining chemical composition, structure, and finding forms of life. Soil intake usually involves cores taken from depths of several meters with a core diameter of 50 mm. Traditional rotary drilling is impractical for low-gravity environments due to the high clamping force required, the heating of the drilling zone (which volatilizes water), and high energy consumption. Ultrasonic drilling is proposed as an alternative because it requires less pressing force, excludes heating, and can drill any material.

## 2. Construction Description
The developed device combines three actions:
1. **Ultrasonic frequency oscillations:** ~20 kHz with an amplitude of ~70 µm.
2. **Low-frequency macro-oscillations:** Induced by a loading mass rebound, with amplitudes ten times higher than the ultrasonic vibrations.
3. **Pseudo-rotational motion:** Provides periodic motion of the construction to increase cutting productivity.

## 3. Results of the Experiments
Testing was performed on silicate brick, red brick, and foam concrete under various modes: Rotation (RD), Ultrasound (UD), Ultrasound with Low-Frequency Vibrations (UD+LFV), Ultrasound with Rotation (UD+RD), and Combined (UD+LFV+RD).

## 4. Conclusion
The combined action (ultrasonic + low-frequency vibrations + rotation) provided a drilling speed increase of up to 49% compared to pure ultrasonic drilling. The maximum drilling speed was achieved in foam concrete (154 mm/min). This system enables continuous control of the processed media for defining soil characteristics during drilling.

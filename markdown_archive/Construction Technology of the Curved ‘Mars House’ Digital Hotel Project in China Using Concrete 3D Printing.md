# Construction Technology of the Curved ‘Mars House’ Digital Hotel Project in China Using Concrete 3D Printing\n\n## Abstract\n3D printing technology is one of the most likely construction technologies to be used when building extraterrestrial bases in the future. The Mars 1 Base project in Gansu Province, China simulates the Martian landscape and uses concrete 3D printing technology to construct houses. In June 2022, the 3D printing construction of five Martian hotel buildings began. The 3D-printed hotel is egg-shaped, with a total height of 9 m and a floor area of 46 square meters. This article introduces the structural design, 3D printing process and overall installation and construction of the Mars house.\n\n## 1 Introduction\nThe advancement of Mars exploration has sparked interest in Mars colonization and habitat development. Concrete 3D printing technology presents a promising approach to constructing Martian habitats, addressing challenges such as extreme environments and limited resources.\n\n## 2 Architectural Design and Structural Design of Mars House\nThe egg-shaped structure was chosen to withstand harsh Martian conditions (strong winds, radiation, temperature differences). The house is 9 m high, divided into two floors. It uses a concrete 3D printed wall as a permanent outer shell/mould, with a steel mesh arranged inside. C40 self-compacting concrete is poured inside the mould to form a reinforced composite structure.\n\n## 3 3D Printing Construction Technology and Process\n### 3.1 Process\nIncludes model refinement, factory printing of components, embedding lifting points, hoisting, and on-site assembly.\n\n### 3.2 Personnel, Materials, and Machines\n- **Printers**: 2 industrial-grade concrete 3D printers (14.8 m x 8.6 m x 5.5 m).\n- **Print Parameters**: Layer height 15 mm, speed 120 mm/s, extrusion width 40 mm.\n- **Material Volume**: 10.6 m3 of printed material and 17.76 m3 of filling concrete per house.\n\n### 3.3 Concrete Mix Proportion and Performance\n| Raw materials | OPC42.5 cement/% | Artificial sand/% | Admixture/% | Water/% |\n| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |\n| Mix proportion | 38 | 42 | 4 | 16 |\n\n**Mechanical Properties:**\n- Compressive strength (printed): 40.3 MPa.\n- Interlayer tensile strength: 3.2 MPa.\n- Permeability grade: P12.\n\n### 3.4 Prefabrication\nComponents are printed as 4 ring-shaped structures. It takes 6 days to complete one house's modules.\n\n## 4 On-Site Assembly and Construction\nComponents are transported 1800 km to the site. A crane assembles the four ring-shaped structures. Structural columns are formed by welding reinforcement cages at junctions and pouring mortar.\n\n## 5 Summary\nThe project demonstrated that concrete 3D printing can greatly improve construction speed and accuracy. It is the world's largest egg-shaped 3D printed structure, providing technical experience for future additive intelligent construction in extreme environments.

# Brazing of SiC ceramic with extraterrestrial regolith simulant in ISRU and ISRF applications

**Authors:** Wei Zheng, Guofu Qiao
**Journal:** Advances in Space Research 70 (2022) 752–761
**Published:** Elsevier, 2022

## Abstract
In-situ resources play an important role in future extraterrestrial human activities for facility repair and habitat construction. This paper verifies the feasibility of lunar and Martian regolith simulant for ceramic joining. SiC ceramic samples were brazed with the regolith under an argon atmosphere. The results show that it is possible to braze ceramic parts together with extraterrestrial regolith simulant. The average ultimate shear strength of joints was 21.5 ± 6.5 MPa (Lunar) and 27.3 ± 13.4 MPa (Martian).

## 1. Introduction
Establishing a permanent lunar base requires raw materials for equipment maintenance and infrastructure construction. Launching resources from Earth is cost-prohibitive. In-situ resource utilization (ISRU) and in-situ fabrication and repair (ISFR) technologies can reduce mass, cost, and risk. High-temperature bonding of regolith is a simple process requiring only heat and the material itself.

## 2. Experimental Procedures
### 2.1. Materials
*   **Regolith Simulants:** HIT-L-1 (Lunar) and HIT-M-1 (Martian).
*   **Ceramics:** Pressureless sintering SiC ceramics (98.5% purity).

### 2.2. Brazing Process
*   **Temperature:** 1400 °C.
*   **Holding Time:** 60 min.
*   **Atmosphere:** Argon.
*   **Heating Rate:** 10 °C/min.

## 3. Results and Discussion
### 3.1. Shear Strength
*   **SiC-LRS (Lunar):** 21.5 ± 6.5 MPa.
*   **SiC-MRS (Martian):** 27.3 ± 13.4 MPa.

### 3.2. Microstructure and Mechanism
The joining mechanism involves molten glass derived from the regolith infiltrating the pores of the SiC ceramic. A reaction layer forms consisting of TiC and Fe3Si through direct atomic bonding.

## 4. Conclusions
This study verifies the feasibility of brazing SiC ceramic with extraterrestrial regolith simulant for ISRU and ISRF applications on the Moon and Mars. The joints achieved significant shear strength, suitable for equipment repair and structural fabrication.

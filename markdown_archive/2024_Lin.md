## Abstract
3D printing technology is one of the most likely construction technologies to be used when building extraterrestrial bases in the future. The Mars 1 Base project in Gansu Province, China simulates the Martian landscape and uses concrete 3D printing technology to construct houses. In June 2022, the 3D printing construction of five Martian hotel buildings began. The 3D-printed hotel is egg-shaped, with a total height of 9 m and a floor area of 46 square meters. After the concrete components are 3D printed in the factory, they are transported to the project site for assembly and construction. This project is currently the world’s largest egg-shaped 3D printed structure.

## 1 Introduction
Advancements in Mars exploration have sparked interest in Mars colonization and habitat development. Concrete 3D printing technology presents a promising approach to constructing Martian habitats, addressing challenges like extreme environments and limited resources through innovative building methods and in-situ resource utilization (ISRU) strategies.

## 2 Architectural Design and Structural Design of Mars House
The egg-shaped structure was chosen to better withstand harsh Martian conditions, such as strong winds, radiation, and high-temperature differences. 
- **Height:** 9 m
- **Floor Area:** 46 square meters (two floors)
- **Features:** Triangular windows, top skylight, segmented 3D printing model (four rings).
- **Structure:** Concrete 3D printed wall as the outer shell/permanent mould, with a steel mesh arranged inside. C40 self-compacting concrete is poured inside to form a reinforced composite structure.

## 3 3D Printing Construction Technology and Process
### 3.1 3D Printing Process
The process includes model refinement, material/equipment preparation, component printing in a factory, pouring concrete, hoisting, transportation (1800 km), and on-site assembly.

### 3.2 Personnel, Materials, and Machines
- **Printers:** 2 industrial-grade concrete 3D printers.
- **Dimensions:** 14.8 m × 8.6 m × 5.5 m.
- **Print Parameters:** Single layer height: 15 mm; Speed: 120 mm/s; Extrusion width: 40 mm.
- **Materials:** 10.6 m³ of printing material and 17.76 m³ of internal filling concrete per house.

### 3.3 3D Printed Concrete Mix Proportion and Performance
| Raw materials | OPC42.5 cement/% | Artificial sand/% | Admixture/% | Water/ % |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Mix proportion | 38 | 42 | 4 | 16 |

**Performance:**
- **Workability:** 200 mm
- **Setting Time:** 41 min
- **Compressive Strength (Printed):** 40.3 MPa (16% reduction from standard molding at 47.9 MPa).
- **Interlayer Tensile Strength:** 3.2 MPa.
- **Permeability Grade:** P12.

## 4 Mar House On-Site Assembly and Construction
Printed wall components were assembled using a crane into four ring-shaped structures. Over 10 sets of steel cables were used for lifting. The installation of the four rings for a single house took one day to complete.

## 5 Summary
The successful application of concrete 3D printing in the Mars habitat hotel project improved construction speed and provided technical experience for large egg-shaped 3D printed structures, promoting the development of additive intelligent construction.

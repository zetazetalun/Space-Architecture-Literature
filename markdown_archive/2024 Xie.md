# The first biological experiment on lunar surface for Humankind: Device and results

**Journal:** Acta Astronautica 214 (2024) 216–223
**Authors:** GengXin Xie, JingHang Ding, YuanXun Zhang, MaoZhi Ren, Dan Qiu, Xi Wang

## Abstract
The Bioregenerative Life Support System (BLSS) is an indispensable part of extraterrestrial bases as it can provide food, oxygen, and waste recycling. The Chinese Chang’e 4 lunar lander carried the Biological Experiment Payload (BEP), a 2.608 kg micro-ecosystem, to the lunar surface. The experiment included seeds of cotton, potato, Arabidopsis, and rape, alongside fly eggs and yeast. Results showed that cotton seeds germinated faster on the Moon (22 h) than on Earth (53 h), though the seedlings exhibited altered morphology due to the 1/6 g gravity and radiation environment.

## 1. Introduction
Lunar bases must be self-sufficient due to high transportation costs. BLSS is a critical technology for these bases. Previous facilities like BIOS-3 and Biosphere-2 demonstrated high system closure on Earth, but the effects of the lunar environment (low gravity, radiation) on such systems remained uncertain until the Chang'e 4 mission.

## 2. The Biological Experiment Payload (BEP)
### 2.1 Design and Specifications
- **Weight:** 2.608 kg
- **Dimensions:** 173 mm (diameter) x 198 mm (height)
- **Internal Space:** 0.82 L total (0.42 L bio-activity space)
- **Sealing:** Air leakage rate < 1.9 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·cm²/s
- **Structure:** Cylindrical aluminum shell with multilayer insulation and thermoelectric coolers (TEC).
- **Components:** Water storage (18 ml), CCD cameras, light guide tube for natural sunlight, sensors for temperature and pressure.

### 2.2 Biological Components
- **Producers:** Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum), Potato (Solanum tuberosum), Arabidopsis thaliana, Rape (Brassica napus).
- **Consumer:** Drosophila melanogaster.
- **Decomposer:** Saccharomyces fermentati.
- **Substrate:** Vermiculite.

## 3. Ground Testing
Preparatory tests on Earth simulated lunar light intensity, thermal equilibrium, and sealing. Biological samples were tested for temperature tolerance (0°C to 40°C) and radiation resistance (X-ray exposure at 10⁴-10⁶ times lunar intensity). Developmental retardation (diapause) was achieved for 90 days prior to landing through humidity and temperature control.

## 4. Results on the Lunar Surface
- **Landing:** Von Kármán crater, January 3, 2019.
- **Germination:** Cotton seed germinated 22 h after watering (Earth synchronization took 53 h).
- **Morphology:** Lunar seedlings showed curved stems, fat streamlined nodes close to the substrate, and curled leaves.
- **Survival:** Seedlings remained upright and green after the first lunar night (temperatures reaching -52°C inside the payload), whereas Earth-based control samples died under similar cold conditions.
- **Operation:** The system worked for 1300 hours over five lunar days before decommissioning on May 9, 2019.

## 5. Discussion
The experiment proved that plant cultivation is possible on the Moon using natural light. Key future research directions include:
- Improving lunar soil for cultivation (ISRU).
- Utilizing lunar rocks as construction materials.
- Utilizing lunar lava tubes for base construction.

# Micromechanical testing and property upscaling of planetary rocks: A critical review  ## Abstract Knowledge of the mechanical behavior of planetary rocks is indispensable for space explorations. This review discusses advances in microscale rock mechanical experiments (micro-RMEs) and upscaling methods for extracting mechanical parameters to support planetary defense, sample return missions, and extraterrestrial construction.  ## 1. Introduction Understanding mechanical properties (compressive strength, elasticity, toughness) is crucial for landing, sampling, and establishing space research stations. These properties guide in-situ resource utilization (ISRU) technologies and material selection for construction.  ## 2. Methods of Mineralogical and Microstructural Analyses - **Microscopy:** SEM and TEM are used for surface topography and atomic-level imaging. - **EDS:** Identifies elemental composition and minerals. - **X-ray microscopy (XCT):** Provides 3D internal structure and porosity data. - **XRD:** Determines crystalline structure and mineral phases. - **EPMA:** High-resolution chemical composition analysis.  ## 3. Micromechanical Experiments - **Nanoindentation:** Measures hardness and elastic modulus using controlled loads on small samples. Tables 1 and 2 summarize data for asteroid, lunar, and Martian meteorites. - **Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM):** Provides nanoscale topography and mechanical properties like adhesion and Young's modulus of mineral interfaces.  ## 4. Upscaling of Microscale Mechanical Properties - **Homogenization Schemes:** Includes Mori-Tanaka (M-T), Self-consistent (SCS), and Voigt-Reuss-Hill (VRH) methods to estimate macroscale rock behavior from micro-measurements. - **Accurate Grain-Based Modeling (AGBM):** A 2D/3D numerical simulation technique that incorporates real microstructure (via TIMA) and micro-RME data to predict macroscale response.  ## 5. Prospective Applications - **Sample Return:** Designing drilling/coring tools based on rock strength. - **Planetary Defense:** Modeling asteroid response to kinetic impacts or nuclear deflection. - **Extraterrestrial Construction:** Assessing regolith-based construction materials (e.g., cold sintering, laser sintering, 3D printing) to ensure structural reliability in extreme environments.  ## 6. Conclusions Non-destructive micro-RMEs have made significant strides in providing insights into planetary materials, which are vital for future deep space exploration and habitat construction.
